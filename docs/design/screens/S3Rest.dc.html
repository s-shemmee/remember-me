Questions, answered

Is Remember Me free?+

Yes. People, dates, reminders and wishlists are free.

How do I sign up?+

With your Google or Apple account in one tap, or with your email. No social media needed.

Do my friends need an account?+

No. You can add anyone. If they join, you connect and see each other's shared wishlists.

Will my friend know I reserved their gift?+

Never. Reservations are hidden from the wishlist owner. Other friends only see that it's taken.

Does it work on my phone?+

Yes. Add it to your home screen from the browser and get notifications. Android and iPhone apps are on the way.
