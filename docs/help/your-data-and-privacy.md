# Your data and privacy

Your people, days, wishes, plans and notes are private to you. We never sell them or use them for ads. The full details are in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## The people you add

The details you keep about your friends stay with you: they aren't told, and nobody else can see them, not even your connections.

## Download your data

In **Settings → Your data**, choose **Download my data** to get everything in one file: your profile, people and their days, wishes, plans, connections and the gifts you reserved.

## Delete your account

In **Settings → Your data**, choose **Delete my account**. To be sure it's you, type your email (and your password, if you use one). Everything is deleted at once and can't be brought back, so download your data first if you'd like a copy.

## Cookies

We only use what the app needs: staying signed in, your theme, and a few small helpers. No tracking cookies and no ads.
