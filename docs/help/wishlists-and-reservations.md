# Wishlists and secret reservations

Keep track of what you'd love for your birthday, and see what your friends would love, with secret reservations so no gift is bought twice.

## Your two lists

Everyone has two wishlists in **Wishlists**:

- **My birthday:** shared with your connections, so they know what you'd love.
- **Treat yourself:** private, just for you.

## Add a wish

Tap **Add a wish** and add a title, and if you like a link, a price, a note and a photo. Mark how much you want it: **Love it**, **Like it** or **Nice to have**. To change the order, open a wish's options and choose **Move earlier** or **Move later**.

Photos are made smaller on your device before they're uploaded, so location details never leave your phone.

## Secret reservations

In **Wishlists → Friends' wishlists**, you see the birthday wishlists of your connections. Tap **Reserve it** on a gift you plan to give:

- you see "Reserved by you";
- your other friends see "Already taken", so nobody buys the same thing;
- the person whose wish it is never finds out who reserved it, or that anyone did.

Changed your mind? Cancel your reservation and the wish is free again.

## If a reserved wish is removed

If your friend removes a wish you'd reserved, you get a quiet note in the app, so you're not left wondering.

## Your plan ticks itself

When you reserve a gift for a friend, the "Gift" step in your birthday plan for them is ticked for you. See [Plans and Celebrate Me](/help/plans-and-celebrate-me).
