# Reminders by email and push

Noor reminds you before each day, so nothing sneaks up on you.

## Choose when

In **Settings → Reminders**, pick any of:

- 2 weeks before;
- 7 days before;
- 3 days before;
- the day before;
- the morning of.

Reminders arrive at 9:00 in the morning, in your time zone. If you pick none, you won't get reminders.

## By email

Email reminders are on by default. To check they reach you, use **Send me a test email** in Settings. If it doesn't arrive, look in your spam folder and mark it as "not spam". Every reminder email has a link to unsubscribe in one tap.

## Push notifications

You can also get reminders as notifications on your phone or computer:

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications** and choose **Turn on notifications**.
2. Allow notifications when your browser asks.
3. Try **Send a test notification**.

On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work once Remember Me is on your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later).

## Install the app

In **Settings → App**, choose **Install app**. On iPhone and iPad, tap the Share button in Safari, choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

## Your own birthday

Reminders about your own day are separate: turn on **Remind me about my own day** in Celebrate Me.
