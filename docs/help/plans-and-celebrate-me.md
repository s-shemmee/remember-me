# Plans and Celebrate Me

Plan each birthday step by step, and make your own day special too.

## Birthday plans

For anyone's next birthday, tap **Plan their day** (on Home or on their profile in People). A plan is a short checklist: Gift, Message, Call, Cake and Something else. You can tick, rename, add or remove steps, and add a note.

Each year starts a fresh plan, and past plans stay, so you can look back. Plans are private: only you can see them.

When you reserve a gift from a friend's wishlist, the "Gift" step for them is ticked for you.

## Celebrate Me

**Celebrate Me** is about your own birthday:

- a countdown to your day, with a word from Noor;
- **a plan for your day**: plan something with friends, cake, treat yourself, something just for me;
- **your birthday wishlist**, and **Share with my people** to send it;
- **Treat yourself**, your private list;
- **Remind me about my own day**: an email a week before, and a happy birthday message on the day.

## A note to future me

Write a note to yourself. It stays sealed until your next birthday: not even you can read it before then. If you change your mind, choose **Throw away and write a new one**.

## Coming later

"Remind my people" and "Birthday memories" are shown as Coming soon. They aren't available yet.
