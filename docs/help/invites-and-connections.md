# Invites and connections

Connect with the people you love who also use Remember Me, to see each other's birthdays and birthday wishlists.

## Send an invite

You can invite someone from:

- **My profile → Invite your people**: your personal link, with **Copy** next to it;
- a person's profile in People, with **Invite [their name] to Remember Me** (once they accept, they're linked to that person);
- **Celebrate Me → Share with my people**, which shares your personal link with your birthday wishlist.

**Your personal link** stays the same, doesn't expire, and anyone you send it to can connect with you. If it ends up somewhere you didn't mean, use **Reset link**: the old link stops working at once and you get a new one.

**An invite for one of your people** works once, for 7 days, and links them to that person when they accept. Inviting the same person again gives you the same link while it's still open.

Send links however you like: a message, an email, WhatsApp.

## When someone joins through your link

If they joined through your personal link, People asks: **"[Name] joined through your link. Is this someone you already added?"** (the bell says it too). Pick them from your people and **Link**, or **No, add as new**. Nothing is matched for you.

Linking keeps what you wrote: the name you gave them, notes, little things and dates stay yours. You see their photo and their birthday wishlist. If their profile shows a different birthday, you're asked whether to use theirs.

You can also link someone later: **Edit → Their account → Link to account**. If that account was already linked to another of your people, the two are merged: days, plans and little things move to the one you're editing. **Unlink** keeps your person and removes the link.

## Accept an invite

Open the link and accept. If you don't have an account yet, sign up first: the invite waits for you.

## What a connection sees

Once you're connected, you each see:

- the other's first name and look;
- their birthday (day and month), unless they've hidden it;
- their **My birthday** wishlist.

Never your birth year, email, people, plans or notes. Your **Treat yourself** list stays private.

## Hide your birthday

In **Settings → Privacy**, turn off **Show my birthday to my connections**. **My profile → Who can see what** shows a summary and links there.

## Remove a connection

You can remove a connection at any time. Reservations between you are removed too, without a note.
