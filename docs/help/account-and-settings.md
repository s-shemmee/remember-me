# Account and settings

Change how you look, how the app looks, and how you sign in.

## Your profile

In **Profile → About you**, change your name, your birthday, your time zone and your look (initials and colour, a character, or a photo).

## Light, dark or system

In **Settings → Appearance**, choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. You can also switch between light and dark with the moon and sun button at the top of the app.

## Change your email or password

In **Settings → Account**:

- **Change email:** we send a confirmation link to your new address (and a note to your current one).
- **Change password:** enter your current password, then the new one. If you sign in with Google, there's no password to change.

## Forgot your password

On the log-in page, choose **Forgot password?** and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

## Log out everywhere

In **Settings → Account**, choose **Log out of all devices**. This also stops notifications on every device.
