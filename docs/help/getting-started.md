# Getting started

Remember Me helps you remember the people you love: their birthdays and special days, the little things they like, their wishlists, and your own day too. Here's how to begin.

## Create your account

Sign up with Google, or with your email and a password (at least 8 characters). With email, we send you a link to confirm your address first. Remember Me is free.

## The first minute

After signing up, Noor (the little candle) asks a few quick things:

- your first name and your birthday (the year is optional);
- how you'd like to look: your initials, a character or a photo;
- your first person, if you'd like to add one now (you can skip it);
- when you'd like reminders.

You can change all of it later, in Profile and Settings.

## Find your way around

- **Home:** who's coming up next, your birthday plans, and a word from Noor.
- **People:** everyone you've added, with their days and little things.
- **Calendar:** every day worth remembering, month by month.
- **Wishlists:** your own lists, and the birthday wishlists your friends share with you.
- **Celebrate Me:** your own birthday, with a countdown and a plan.

## Use it like an app

You can install Remember Me on your phone from the browser, so it opens like any other app. See [Reminders by email and push](/help/reminders) for how.
