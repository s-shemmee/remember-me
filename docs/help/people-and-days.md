# People and their days

Everyone you add lives in **People**: their name, how you know them, a colour, their days and the little things they love.

## Add someone

Tap **Add someone**, then fill in:

- their name;
- how you know them (Best friend, Family, Partner, Friend or Colleague), and if you like, a few words of your own, like "Little sister";
- their birthday (the year is optional; with it, Remember Me shows how old they're turning);
- little things they love, like "mint tea" or "vinyl records".

## More than birthdays

Tap **Add another day** to add an anniversary, a graduation, a work milestone, a remembrance day, or any other day with your own title. Days can come back every year, or happen once (then they need a year).

## Little things

Little things help you choose gifts and remember what makes someone smile. Noor uses them in your reminders too.

Each little thing has an emoji. I suggest one as you type (coffee gets ☕, football gets ⚽), or tap it to choose another, type any emoji you like, or go back to the heart.

## In memory

If someone has passed away, turn on **In memory** in their profile. Their days are then remembered gently: no countdowns, no "turns 70", and no gift ideas.

## Edit or remove someone

Open a person and choose **Edit** to change anything. If you remove someone, you can undo it straight away. After 30 days, a removed person is deleted for good, with their days.

## Calendar

**Calendar** shows every day of every person, month by month, with the next 30 days listed beside it.
