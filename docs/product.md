# Product

## Why
Friends forgot my birthday for years. Not because they didn't care: life is loud. Remember Me is a quiet helper that makes it easy to show up for the people you love, and reminds you that your own day matters too.

## Who
People who care but forget. Mostly on phones, often non-technical, in Morocco and beyond. English first.

## Core loop
1. Add a person (name, relation, their days, little things they love).
2. Noor reminds you before the day (7 days, 1 day, the morning of, by default).
3. You open their wishlist, secretly reserve a gift, write a wish, celebrate.
4. They feel remembered. Maybe they join, connect, and share their own wishlist.

## Features (v1 web)
- **Landing page:** hero with rotating words, what's inside, how it works, meet Noor, FAQ, final CTA, footer.
- **Auth:** sign up and log in with Google, Apple or email. Forgot password. Email confirmation.
- **Onboarding (4 steps):** your birthday → your look (avatar, skippable) → your first person (skippable) → reminders → done.
- **Home:** greeting, Noor's nudge with an action, 4 stat cards, coming up list with filters, next person's plan, mini month.
- **People:** grid + filters + search, profile panel (days, little things, their wishlist), connection requests.
- **Calendar:** month grid with name tags, day details, next 30 days.
- **Wishlists:** mine (birthday list + private "treat yourself"), friends' (reserve secretly, undo).
- **Celebrate Me:** countdown, my plan checklist, treat yourself, note to future me (opens on the day), remind my people, memories.
- **Profile:** your card, invite link, about you, who sees what, connections.
- **Settings:** reminders, notifications, privacy, appearance (light/dark/system, show Noor, reduce motion), account (email, password, providers, export data, delete).
- **Account menu** in the sidebar: profile, settings, help, log out.
- **PWA:** installable, offline shell, push notifications.

## Principles
- Gentle, never pushy. Notifications are few and useful.
- Private by default. Reservations are secret, forever.
- Works for everyone: accessible, fast on cheap phones, clear in light and dark.
- Fun to use: playful to act, calm to show.

## Out of scope for v1
Payments, gift buying inside the app, group chats, social feed, languages other than English (the AI wish writer may still write in French or Darija).
